THE FOUR SIGNALS

Four independent reads. One cross-checked score.

Most tools measure one of these. Undertow measures all four, then tells you where they agree — and where they don't.

C

Crowd

What retail is actually saying — StockTwits, Reddit, and search interest, cross-checked against each other, not trusted on their own.

W

Wall Street

Analyst consensus, price targets, and whether professional sentiment is getting more or less bullish over time.

B

Business

Revenue, margins, cash flow, balance sheet health, and valuation — what's actually happening inside the company.

M

Market

Where the stock already sits relative to its own range and trend — is the story already priced in, or still fresh.
