Building the team behind Undertow.

Undertow is growing beyond where it started. The roles below aren't posted yet, but they're what's coming next — and what we'll be looking for when they open.

Whoever joins won't be one of thousands buried in a roadmap they never see — with a team this size, every hire genuinely shapes what Undertow becomes next. The data engineer who joins picks the next real signal to add; the designer who joins sets the visual language for pages that don't exist yet. We're looking for people who'd rather ship something small and real this week than sit in a planning meeting about something big and hypothetical next quarter. That's honestly the fun part of a team this size: watching something you built go live within days, judged by whether it's actually useful, not by whose name is on the deck. If that sounds better than disappearing into a big team, you'll probably like it here.
