ABOUT

A market intelligence system built to show its work.

Undertow is an independent, personal project — not a fund, not a data vendor. It exists because single-source sentiment tools and single-number scores weren't good enough to trust.

01

The problem

Most "retail sentiment" tools read one feed and present a bullish/bearish score with total confidence. Most "AI stock scores" fuse everything — sentiment, analysts, fundamentals — into a single opaque number. Neither tells you when the sources actually disagree, which is usually the most important thing to know.

Undertow started from a specific frustration: wanting to know what retail was thinking, what Wall Street expected, what the business actually showed, and whether the market had already priced it in — and not trusting any single answer enough to act on it alone.

"If four independent reads agree, that's worth something. If they don't, that's worth knowing too — and worth knowing why."
02

The approach

Undertow scores four independent pillars, then a synthesis layer explains where they agree and where they don't. A Divergence Engine classifies real disagreement into named patterns, not a generic "these differ" flag. Every score traces back to real, live data — nothing is asserted without showing where it came from.

Crowd

StockTwits, Reddit, and Google Trends — cross-checked against each other, not taken as one voice.

Wall Street

Analyst consensus and price targets, with its own higher divergence bar since sell-side ratings skew bullish.

Business

Real fundamentals — growth, profitability, cash flow, balance sheet, valuation — transparently scored.

Market

Momentum and how much strength is already priced in — not a verdict, context for the other three.

How it's built

A Python script runs once a day on GitHub Actions' free tier, pulls from the sources below, scores sentiment and fundamentals, cross-checks them against real price action, and writes a static dashboard served by GitHub Pages. No server to maintain, no database, no subscription — which also means no incentive to gate features behind a paywall.

04

What it isn't

  • Not financial advice. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell.
  • Not a trading signal service. Divergence signals highlight disagreement, not predictions.
  • Not a black box. Every score traces back to a visible, real source.

A quick disclaimer

Undertow is for personal, non-commercial use. Nothing on this site is investment advice. Prices may be delayed. Do your own research.

What we hold to
