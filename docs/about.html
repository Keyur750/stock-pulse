ABOUT

A market intelligence system built to show its work.

Undertow is an independent, personal project — not a fund, not a data vendor. It exists because single-source sentiment tools and single-number scores weren't good enough to trust.

The problem

Most "retail sentiment" tools read one feed and present a bullish/bearish score with total confidence. Most "AI stock scores" fuse everything — sentiment, analysts, fundamentals — into a single opaque number. Neither tells you when the sources actually disagree, which is usually the most important thing to know.

Undertow started from a specific frustration: wanting to know what retail was thinking, what Wall Street expected, what the business actually showed, and whether the market had already priced it in — and not trusting any single answer enough to act on it alone.

"If four independent reads agree, that's worth something. If they don't, that's worth knowing too — and worth knowing why."

The approach

Undertow scores four independent pillars — Crowd (StockTwits, Reddit, Google Trends), Wall Street (analyst consensus and price targets), Business (real fundamentals), and Market (momentum and how much is already priced in) — then a synthesis layer explains where they agree and where they don't. A Divergence Engine classifies real disagreement into named patterns, not a generic "these differ" flag.

Every score traces back to real, live data. Nothing is asserted without showing where it came from.

How it's built

A Python script runs once a day on GitHub Actions' free tier, pulls from StockTwits, Reddit, ApeWisdom, Google Trends, SEC EDGAR, and Yahoo Finance, scores sentiment and fundamentals, cross-checks them against real price action, and writes a static dashboard served by GitHub Pages. No server to maintain, no database, no subscription — which also means no incentive to gate features behind a paywall.

What it isn't

Not financial advice. Not a trading signal service. Not a black box — every score traces back to a visible source. Signals highlight disagreement between sources, not predictions.

A quick disclaimer

Undertow is for personal, non-commercial use. Nothing on this site is investment advice. Prices may be delayed. Do your own research.

What we hold to

Show the work

Every score breaks down by pillar. No unexplained numbers.

Four pillars that don't share a bias by construction.

Free by design

Running on free infrastructure removes the incentive to gate or manipulate.

Disagreement over noise

The Divergence Engine surfaces the handful of things worth a second look.
