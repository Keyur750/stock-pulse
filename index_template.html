THE FOUR SIGNALS

Four independent reads. One cross-checked score.

Most tools measure one of these. Undertow measures all four, then tells you where they agree — and where they don't.

C
Crowd
—/100

Retail sentiment is loud but often wrong on its own. We cross-check StockTwits, Reddit, and Google search interest against each other — three independent crowds instead of one — before calling anything a real signal.

W
Wall Street
—/100

Analyst ratings move slowly, which makes the direction more useful than the number itself. We track whether professional sentiment is getting more or less bullish over time, not just where it happens to sit today.

B
Business
—/100

Revenue, margins, cash flow, balance sheet health, and valuation — the company underneath the ticker. It's the slowest-moving of the four signals, and usually the most durable one.

M
Market
—/100

Price already knows things the other three signals haven't caught up to yet. This measures momentum and extension relative to the stock's own range — whether the move looks fresh, or already stretched.
