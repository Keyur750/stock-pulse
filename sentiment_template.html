Crowd sentiment across the securities Undertow covers — what it is, how it's changing, and what's behind it.

Each security's score blends two sources. When a trader self-tags their own StockTwits post as Bullish or Bearish, that stated opinion counts as ground truth and is weighted twice as heavily as an inferred read. Everything else — untagged StockTwits posts and Reddit chatter — is classified by an AI model reading the actual message text for real meaning, including sarcasm and negation, not just keyword matches. A VADER lexicon model is used only as an offline fallback if the AI call is unavailable.

Mention counts reflect real message volume from the last 24 hours (up to a safety ceiling), not a fixed sample size. Sentiment scoring itself runs on the most recent 60 messages per security to keep AI usage bounded. Confidence reflects how many of the 4 intelligence pillars (crowd, Wall Street, business, market) have real data for a security.

This is not financial advice. It reflects retail chatter, not fundamentals or professional analysis.
